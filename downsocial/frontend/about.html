About downsocial — Free All-in-One Video & Audio Downloader

Welcome to downsocial.net. We built this platform with one straightforward mission: give everyone a fast, secure, and effortless way to download videos, reels, shorts, and MP3 audio from Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat, and Threads — without watermarks, without sign-ups, and 100% free.

Our Mission

We believe that saving publicly available videos for offline learning, travel, and personal memories should be simple and private. Existing downloaders are often cluttered with spam, demand software installations, or degrade video quality. downsocial eliminates all friction with a clean, fast, and secure browser-based tool.

Multi-Platform Support

downsocial handles all your favorite platforms in one place: Facebook, Instagram Reels, TikTok (no watermark), YouTube Shorts, Snapchat Spotlights, and Threads. You can choose crisp 1080p HD MP4 video or crystal-clear 192kbps MP3 audio.

Zero Tracking & Total Privacy

We operate on a zero-knowledge architecture. No account registration, no cookies tracking your downloads, and no media files stored on our servers. Your downloads remain 100% private and secure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is downsocial really free?

Yes, downsocial is 100% free with unlimited downloads and no hidden subscription fees.

Do I need an account to download videos?

No account registration or login is required. You can download videos immediately and anonymously.

Which platforms are supported?

downsocial supports video and audio downloads from Facebook, Instagram Reels, TikTok (no watermark), YouTube Shorts, Snapchat, and Threads.

Does downsocial store or keep my downloaded videos?

No. We operate on a zero-knowledge architecture and do not host, store, or archive any downloaded media on our servers.
